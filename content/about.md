Cycle Five Syndicate builds software for the people who build software — starting
with us. Every product we ship is one we reach for in our own day-to-day. If we
wouldn't use it, we don't ship it.

We are advocates of **Rust** and **AI-assisted coding**, with human
understanding kept firmly at the center. Tooling and models do the heavy
lifting; people decide what's worth making and why. That combination — fast,
correct systems and thoughtful design — is how we work and what we believe in.

We're a small group that values reliability, versatility, and tools that respect
the person using them.
