You can delete your Anya account and all associated personal data at any time:

**In the app:** open Anya and go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and associated personal data, subject to limited legal retention described in our Privacy Policy.

**By request:** if you can't access the app, email **hello@cyclefive.xyz** from the address associated with your account and request deletion. We will verify your identity and process the deletion.

For details on what data we hold and our retention practices, see our Privacy Policy at [/privacy/](/privacy/).
